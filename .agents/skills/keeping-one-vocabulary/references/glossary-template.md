The vocabulary this repository argues in. One term, one meaning. If a proposal needs a
word that is not here, add it in the same pull request; if it needs a word that is here
to mean something else, that is a conflict — see *Contested terms*.

Terms borrowed from ICON, gt4py, or CF conventions keep their upstream meaning unless
this file says otherwise, and any departure is stated explicitly.

## Terms

| Term | Meaning | Anchored in |
|---|---|---|
| _example_ — Granule | A unit of ICON physics ported to icon4py as a self-contained computation, retaining the Fortran granule's inputs and outputs. Historical; being replaced by *component*. | [[personal/egparedes/layered-architecture-refactor\|Layered architecture]] |

Fill one row per term:

- **Meaning** — one sentence, in the present tense, naming what the thing *is*, not
  what it does for you.
- **Anchored in** — the proposal that establishes the meaning, as a wikilink. A term
  with no anchor is either obvious or not yet agreed.
